Method

How to read this

One site phase, counted once

The same building often appears in a chip vendor's announcement, a cloud provider's and a developer's. Each row here is one tranche of capacity at one place, and companies are roles on that row, so nothing is counted twice.

Three kinds of megawatt

IT load is what the chips draw. Facility load adds cooling and losses and is what most announcements quote. Grid load is the utility connection. Each row keeps the figure as its source states it; where only one is given, the other is derived at a PUE of 1.3 and marked as derived.

Timing

Capacity is placed in the period it energizes, following a staged ramp where sources give one. Half-years are shown in Q2 and Q4. Year-only dates appear in the annual view and in a "quarter not given" column in the quarterly view. Capacity with no date is reported as undated and left out of the chart.

Everything announced counts

The totals include everything that has been announced or planned, from operating halls to letters of intent. Use "Stage" to narrow it: operating, under construction, permitted, or paper stage. Paper stage means announced but not started, reported but unconfirmed, or committed in an agreement with no site yet.

Agreements with no site

Some capacity is committed in an agreement before any site is named. That part is in the totals as its own row, labelled "Agreement, no site". We take the agreement's size, subtract what is already at named sites, and subtract capacity that another agreement already covers. Chip supply agreements are assumed to fill the buyer's named sites first. The location is given by country only: "Country stated" where a source says so, Estimate where it is our estimate from where that company builds. Estimated locations are low confidence, and some of this capacity may sit in a provider's datacenter that is already listed without a named customer. Use "Show" to see named sites only.

What is not counted

Every announced datacenter project and agreement is in the data. An item leaves the totals only when a public source says it was cancelled, dropped or replaced; it stays on record with that source and can be picked under "Stage". Power equipment for sites that are already listed (fuel cells, generators, supply contracts) adds nothing. A planned campus whose only published figure is its power supply is counted, with that figure as an upper bound. Where a chip deal and a site describe the same capacity, it is counted once, and the deals table shows how much of each deal is already counted at sites. Where a grid connection is the only published figure, it is counted as an upper bound and labelled.

Operator and end user

The operator runs the facility or the cloud inside it; the end user is whose workloads run there. Both are taken only from what sources state. Where no source names one, the capacity sits in a "Not disclosed" row at the foot of the table rather than assigned by inference.

Sources and status

Every row links to at least one public source: a company release, filing, earnings call, utility or permit document, or press report. "Verified" rows have a supporting quote on file; "Sourced" rows have a matching public source and are awaiting a full read. Estimates are labelled.
