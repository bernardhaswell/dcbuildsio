How to read this

One site phase, counted once

The same building often appears in a chip vendor's announcement, a cloud provider's and a developer's. Each row here is one tranche of capacity at one place, and companies are roles on that row, so nothing is counted twice.

Three kinds of megawatt

IT load is what the chips draw. Facility load adds cooling and losses and is what most announcements quote. Grid load is the utility connection. Each row keeps the figure as its source states it; where only one is given, the other is derived at a PUE of 1.3 and marked as derived.

Timing

Capacity is placed in the period it energizes, following a staged ramp where sources give one. Half-years are shown in Q2 and Q4. Year-only dates appear in the annual view and in a "quarter not given" column in the quarterly view. Capacity with no date is reported as undated and left out of the chart.

Sources and status

Every row links to at least one public source: a company release, filing, earnings call, utility or permit document, or press report. "Verified" rows have a supporting quote on file; "Sourced" rows have a matching public source and are awaiting a full read. Estimates are labelled.

Data compiled from public sources. Figures are announcements and estimates, not guarantees, and change often. Corrections are welcome through the repository's issue tracker.
